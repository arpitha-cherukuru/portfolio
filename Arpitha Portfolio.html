FraudOps — finding risk without overwhelming the team.

How can we identify payments that merit attention while keeping the investigation workload practical?

Approach

Used Python and SQL to analyse 1.2M+ simulated payments, developing logistic regression risk classification with time-based validation.

Operational focus

Designed a cost-aware decision framework and simulated 47,000+ investigations with service-level and capacity constraints; built three Power BI monitoring dashboards.

What I'd validate before deployment ↗

This is a simulated payments project, not a deployed banking system. The cost-aware framework considers fraud losses, false positives, investigation costs and capacity; deployment would require model-calibration, drift and fairness evaluation.

SELECTED CASE STUDY · 02PREDICTIVE MODELLING

F1 strategy — knowing when assumptions change.

Comparing alternative race strategies using simulation and Random Forest modelling.

Method

Engineered race-level datasets, compared strategies in simulations, and evaluated a Random Forest predictive model using cross-validation.

Finding

Identified inefficiencies in 31% of analysed strategies with an estimated average time loss of 86 seconds.

What the results mean ↗

These are findings within modelled race scenarios, not verified improvements in real racing-team performance. Strategy recommendations are conditional on assumptions.
